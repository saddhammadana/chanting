# Code of Conduct

Chanting follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies to the issue tracker, pull requests, and any other space where the
project is discussed.

In short:

- Be respectful and considerate. This is a prayer-book app used by people of
  different traditions and backgrounds; treat the content and each other with
  care.
- Criticise ideas and code, not people.
- No harassment, insults, or discriminatory language.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer at
`saddhammadana@gmail.com`. Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may
edit or remove comments, close threads, or block accounts that break these
rules.
